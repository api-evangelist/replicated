October 2025 highlights include firewall opening verification (alpha) in Compatibility Matrix, improved Enterprise Portal access from Vendor Portal, and major usability upgrades to release management and customer views. Replicated also added new tutorials for headless and Helm-based installs, refreshed the quick start guide, and introduced a detailed ConfigValues reference in the docs.
