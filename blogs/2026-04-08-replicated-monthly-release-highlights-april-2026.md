Replicated’s April 2026 release highlights introduce major updates across Embedded Cluster, Vendor Portal, and Enterprise Portal, including Helm v4 support, richer support bundle metadata and API capabilities, and new in-portal bundle exploration tools. Vendors gain enhanced visibility with new event notifications, streamlined onboarding with the ability to set a default app in the Vendor Portal and to send automatic Enterprise Portal invites, and improved Enterprise Portal usability through browser-based air gap bundle downloads and clearer customer access controls.
