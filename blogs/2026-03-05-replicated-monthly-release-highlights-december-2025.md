December 2025 highlights include Helm v4 validation in Vendor Portal and new controls for Helm-based installs. Enterprise Portal adds per-customer SAML authorization (Alpha), expanded Support Engineer RBAC, and automatic air gap record creation. Documentation updates improve clarity on support requests, support bundle processes, and end-user Enterprise Portal tasks.
