A framework for vendors to scale customer health awareness by utilizing the Replicated platform to capture structured data about every customer's journey. The post distinguishes between unreliable proxy signals (like email opens) and crucial behavioral signals (such as application status, version upgrades, and custom metrics) that come directly from the running software.
