Embedded Cluster picks up a persistent admin console and moves onto containerd 2.x with Kubernetes 1.36, KOTS adds Kopia support for modern Velero, and the proxy registry gains IAM and OIDC authentication along with new egress visibility. A few features also reached general availability this month, including Custom Metrics and Compatibility Matrix VMs and airgap environments.
